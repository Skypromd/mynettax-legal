Privacy Policy

MyNetTax · Last updated: 12 August 2026 · Version 1.0

This Privacy Policy explains how Financial Agent Accounting Ltd (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, stores and protects your personal data when you use the MyNetTax application and related services (the “Service”). MyNetTax helps sole traders and Construction Industry Scheme (CIS) subcontractors calculate their tax and submit Making Tax Digital (MTD) for Income Tax updates to HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC).

We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy and are responsible for protecting it. We process your data in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

2. What personal data we collect

We collect only the data we need to provide the Service:

Account and identity data

Name, email address, password (stored only in hashed form), and preferred language.

Tax and financial data

Your National Insurance number (NINO), business details, income and expense figures, CIS deductions, and the figures required to calculate your Income Tax and prepare your MTD submissions.

HMRC authorisation data

When you connect your HMRC account, we receive and store OAuth access and refresh tokens that allow the Service to interact with HMRC on your behalf. We never see or store your HMRC sign-in username or password — you enter those directly on HMRC’s own website.

Technical and device data

Device identifiers, IP address, timezone, and similar information required by HMRC’s Fraud Prevention Header specification, plus basic app usage and error logs needed to keep the Service secure and working.

4. Who we share your data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only where necessary:

  • HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) — to submit your MTD for Income Tax information, at your instruction.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers — who store and process data on our behalf under written data-processing terms, on servers located in the United Kingdom.
  • Authorities or advisers — where we are legally required to disclose, or to establish or defend legal claims.

All processors act only on our documented instructions and are bound by confidentiality and security obligations.

5. Where your data is stored

Your personal data is stored and processed on servers located in the United Kingdom. If at any point we need to transfer data outside the UK, we will ensure an appropriate safeguard recognised under UK GDPR (such as UK adequacy regulations or the International Data Transfer Agreement) is in place, and we will update this policy.

6. How long we keep your data

We keep your tax and financial records for as long as your account is active and for the period required by UK tax law (generally at least 6 years after the relevant tax year), after which it is deleted or anonymised. Account data is deleted when you close your account, except where we must retain limited information to meet a legal obligation.

7. How we protect your data

  • All data is encrypted in transit using TLS.
  • Access tokens and personally identifiable data are encrypted at rest using industry-standard encryption (AES), with encryption keys managed separately from the data.
  • Access is restricted to authenticated users, and each customer can only access their own data.
  • We maintain an audit trail, apply the principle of data minimisation, and review our security controls.

8. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Erase your data (“right to be forgotten”), subject to legal retention duties;
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format;
  • Restrict or object to certain processing;
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

You can exercise these rights in the app or by emailing skypromd@gmail.com. We respond within one month.

9. Complaints

If you have concerns about how we handle your data, please contact us first so we can help. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

10. Cookies

Our web application uses only cookies and similar technologies that are strictly necessary to keep you signed in and the Service secure. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

11. Children

The Service is intended for adults managing their own tax affairs and is not directed at children under 18.
